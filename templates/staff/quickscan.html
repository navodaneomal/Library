How Quick Scan works
  • Scan a library card to start serving that member.
  • Scan a book that is on loan — it is returned instantly.
  • Scan an available book with a member loaded — press Enter to lend it.
  • Esc clears the member. Everything works with a USB scanner; no mouse needed.
